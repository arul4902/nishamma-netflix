# 🎬 NISHAMMA+: A Family Original
> An Original Cinematic Streaming Experience for Nisha Bernard's (Nishamma) Birthday.

---

## 🍿 The Concept
**"Her life is the featured original series."**
- **Her photos** = Cinematic titles & key art
- **Her memories** = Episodes (Malaysian Days, Family First, Grandma Era)
- **Her family** = Cast & ensemble
- **Her personality** = Story & love language
- **Her favorite song** = Official Soundtrack (*Aluva Puzha*)
- **Her birthday tribute** = Season Finale

---

## 🌟 Features
1. **Cinematic Opening (5–8s)**: Glowing laser beam and monogram assembly with programmatic Web Audio API cinematic sound swell.
2. **Profile Selection ("Who's watching?")**: Profiles for Nishamma, Bernard, Aby & Ammu, Aleena & Alen, Kaylu, and Emy.
3. **Cinematic Hero**: 4K UHD metadata, editorial typography, film grain, and subtle parallax zoom.
4. **Continue Watching Shelf**: Responsive streaming carousel with progress bars and episode previews.
5. **Episode 01: The Malaysian Days 🇲🇾**: Kuala Lumpur, KLCC fountains, Genting Highlands, and embedded HD video reel.
6. **Episode 02: Family First**: Interactive cast browser with character roles and relationship bios.
7. **Episode 03: The Overcaring Mom ❤️**: WhatsApp chat simulator with typing animations and simulated incoming call banner.
8. **Episode 04: Breaking News (NNN)**: Nishamma News Network with live broadcast ticker, leaked reel footage, and source fact-checker.
9. **Episode 05: Grandma Era ❤️**: Featuring Kaylu and Emy with warm lighting and emotional storytelling.
10. **Because She Loves... Watchlist**: Netflix-style category rows (Nivin Pauly specials, feel-good movies, family time).
11. **Family Love Meter**: Press-and-hold interactive care index charging up to 999% (`ERROR: TOO MUCH LOVE ❤️`).
12. **Memory Mosaic**: Responsive masonry gallery preserving true aspect ratios with fullscreen lightbox.
13. **Original Soundtrack Player**: *Aluva Puzha* theme song with Canvas-driven dynamic waveform visualizer, audio scrubber, volume slider, and persistent header music pill.
14. **Birthday Finale**: Sequential sentence reveal and complete emotional family tribute letter with confetti.
15. **Closing Credits**: Hollywood-style rolling credits with replay button.
16. **Secret Easter Egg**: 3 clicks on the newspaper icon triggers a family WhatsApp notification flood.
